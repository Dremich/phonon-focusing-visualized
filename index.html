💡 Presentation Key Takeaway: Anisotropy (η) vs. Phonon Focusing (ε)

• Ellipsoidal Anisotropy (η ≠ 1, ε = 0): Steers energy away from phase (vg ∦ q), but the surface stays strictly convex (κ > 0)—rays never cross or form caustics.
• Phonon Focusing (|ε| > 0.06): Creates concave valleys (κ < 0) & inflection points where many q states fold into intense caustic beams!
